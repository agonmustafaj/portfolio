FAQ

Questions businesses in Kosovo ask

How much does a website cost in Kosovo?

It depends on the number of pages, languages and features. A simple business website costs much less than a platform with accounts and payments. Send a short description of the business and I'll reply with a clear price before any work starts.

Why hire a freelancer instead of an agency?

You work directly with the person doing the work, decisions are faster, and you don't pay for agency overhead. The website and the SEO are handled by the same person, so nothing gets lost between teams.

Do you work with businesses outside Prishtina?

Yes. I work with businesses anywhere in Kosovo, from Prizren and Peja to Gjilan and Ferizaj, and with clients abroad. Most of the process happens online.

How long until a new website shows up on Google?

A new site is usually indexed within a few days to a few weeks after launch, once it is submitted in Google Search Console. Ranking for competitive searches takes longer and depends on content, competition and links.

Can you improve the SEO of my existing website?

Yes. I start with a technical and on-page audit, then fix what holds the site back: indexing, speed, titles, page structure and internal links.
