About Me

SEO expert and web developer. Search visibility and websites for businesses, through AgoDev.

I’m Agon Mustafaj, an SEO Expert and Web Developer based in Prishtina, Kosovo. I work at the intersection of search visibility, web development, and AI, helping businesses build a stronger and more effective online presence through AgoDev.

As an SEO Expert at Novalab SEO Agency and a Computer Science student at the University of Prishtina, I combine practical industry experience with a technical understanding of how websites are built, structured, crawled, and discovered.

My work spans technical SEO, on-page optimization, internal linking, site architecture, and search performance. I believe a website should do more than look good. It should be technically sound, easy to navigate, discoverable through search, and built around its purpose.

Through AgoDev, I bring SEO and web development together to create digital experiences where development and search visibility work hand in hand. I also integrate AI tools into my workflow to improve efficiency, accelerate development, and explore better ways to solve technical and creative challenges.

Beyond SEO and development, I have a foundation in Data Analytics, working with Python, Power BI, Excel, DAX, and data modeling through coursework. These skills strengthen my analytical approach and interest in data-informed decisions.

I’m continuously expanding my knowledge across SEO, web technologies, AI, and analytics, with a focus on turning what I learn into practical work, better websites, and measurable improvements.

AgoDev is where I bring these skills together, through services, projects, and ideas focused on building better websites and improving how businesses are found online.
