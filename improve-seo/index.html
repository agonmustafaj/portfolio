Signs

When your SEO needs work

If one or more of these sounds familiar, the website is losing customers to competitors in search.

  • Your business doesn't appear when people search for what you sell
  • Competitors with worse services rank above you
  • Pages are missing from Google or show the wrong title
  • The site is slow, especially on phones
  • Traffic dropped and nobody knows why
  • You have never set up Google Search Console
Process

How I improve your SEO

The same method I use as an SEO Expert at Novalab SEO Agency, applied to your site in five steps.

01

SEO audit

I crawl the whole site with Semrush, Ahrefs and Screaming Frog and check it in Search Console: indexing, errors, speed, titles, duplicate content and links.

02

Technical fixes

Broken pages, redirects, sitemap, robots.txt, canonical tags, mobile layout and loading speed are fixed first, because they block everything else.

03

On-page SEO

Each important page gets a clear title, meta description and heading structure built around what your customers actually search for.

04

Content and internal links

Thin pages get useful content, and pages link to each other so Google understands which ones matter most.

05

Measure and report

Search Console and Google Analytics track clicks, impressions and rankings, and you get a short report on what changed and what comes next.

FAQ

Questions about improving SEO

How long does it take to improve SEO?

Technical fixes like indexing errors, titles and broken links show up in Google within days to a few weeks. Rankings for competitive searches usually take three to six months of steady work, depending on the competition and how many sites link to yours.

Can you guarantee first place on Google?

No, and nobody honest can. Google decides the rankings. What I can promise is that every fix is explained, the work is based on data from your own site, and you see the changes in Search Console.

Do you work with WordPress or other platforms?

Yes. I work with WordPress, custom HTML sites, Next.js and most other platforms. Some fixes need admin access or a developer account, and I tell you exactly what is needed before starting.

What does an SEO audit cost?

It depends on the size of the site and how much needs fixing. Send the website address and I'll reply with a clear price for the audit and the fixes before any work starts.

Can I improve SEO myself instead?

Yes. My practical guide on how to improve website SEO covers search intent, keywords, on-page SEO, technical SEO, links and measurement. Hire help when you don't have the time or when the problems are technical.
