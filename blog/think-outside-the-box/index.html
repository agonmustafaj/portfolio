Think Outside the Box: Adapt Faster, Work Smarter

By Agon Mustafaj · September 10, 2026

The job market is changing really fast. Competition is getting tougher, and every day more people are looking for ways to stand out.

But I believe the biggest advantage won't always go to those who know the most. It will go to those who adapt the fastest.

AI is a tool, not just a threat

AI and automation are changing the way we work. Instead of seeing them only as a threat, we should learn how to use them to our advantage.

Less time spent on repetitive tasks. More time for creativity, analysis, and solving real problems.

That shift matters more than people realize. When routine work gets automated, the value moves to the skills machines don't have: asking the right questions, understanding context, and making judgment calls.

Working smarter

At the end of the day, it's not always about working harder than everyone else. Sometimes, it's about finding a smarter way to work.
