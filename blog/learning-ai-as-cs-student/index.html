Learning AI as a Computer Science Student

By Agon Mustafaj · December 12, 2025

Learning AI while studying Computer Science can feel overwhelming. There are models, tools, frameworks, and new announcements every week. My approach is simple: connect AI to what I am already building.

When I build websites, I explore how AI can support design decisions, content structure, and development speed. When I take courses on data, I look at how machine learning concepts relate to analysis. When I work on SEO, I think about how search and AI systems understand information.

Learn by connecting fields

Web development teaches structure. Data courses teach evidence. SEO teaches discoverability. AI becomes more useful when it sits on top of those skills instead of replacing them.

Keep building

I learn best by experimenting. Reading about AI is useful. Using it inside real projects is better. That is why this portfolio itself is part of my learning process: a place to practice technology, present the work I have built, and keep growing.

AI is part of today’s technology landscape. The students who progress are the ones who stay curious, stay practical, and keep shipping.
