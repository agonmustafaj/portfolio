SEO improvement checklist

Use this order. Skipping technical problems to rewrite titles rarely works.

  1. Confirm search intent for your main queries
  2. Do keyword research tied to business value
  3. Improve on-page SEO (titles, meta, headings, content)
  4. Fix technical SEO (crawl, index, sitemap, mobile, speed)
  5. Strengthen content depth where pages are thin
  6. Build internal links to important pages
  7. Earn relevant backlinks without spam
  8. Improve UX and performance
  9. Measure in Search Console
  10. Decide when to hire vs keep DIY

1. Understand search intent

Before optimizing a website, understand what users are searching for and why. Search intent is the purpose behind a query: learn something, compare options, find a brand, or hire a service.

Someone searching “what is technical SEO” wants an explanation. Someone searching “SEO freelancer Kosovo” wants a person to hire. Matching that intent is the first step to improve SEO performance.

Practical tip: Open Google for your target keyword and shape your page to match what already ranks, then make it clearer and more useful.

2. Conduct keyword research

Keyword research finds the terms your audience uses. Prefer relevance and business value over raw volume.

  • Relevance: Does the keyword match your offer?
  • Search intent: What does the user expect?
  • Competition: Can you realistically rank?
  • Search volume: Is there enough demand?
  • Business value: Will the traffic matter?

Tools such as Google Keyword Planner, Google Search Console, Ahrefs, and Semrush help. Specific queries often convert better than broad head terms.

3. Improve on-page SEO

On-page SEO is how each page’s content and HTML explain the topic to people and search engines. If you are asking how to improve on-page SEO, start here.

Title tags

Write a unique, descriptive title for each important page. Include the primary keyword naturally. Titles that promise a checklist, outcome, or clear angle usually earn more clicks than generic “practical guide” phrasing.

Meta descriptions

Meta descriptions are not a direct ranking factor, but they influence CTR. Summarize the outcome in one or two sentences.

Headings and content

Use one H1, then H2/H3 sections that answer real questions. Put the main answer early. Avoid keyword stuffing.

Practical tip: Review pages with impressions but low CTR in Search Console and rewrite their titles and openings first.

4. Improve technical SEO

Technical SEO is different from on-page SEO. On-page is what the page says. Technical SEO is whether Google can crawl, understand, and index it reliably.

  • Crawlability: Important pages reachable through links and navigation
  • Indexing: No accidental noindex on money pages
  • XML sitemap: Canonical URLs you want discovered
  • Robots.txt: Not blocking key resources
  • Canonical tags: Handling near-duplicates
  • Mobile usability: Readable without zooming
  • Page performance: Faster loads, lean scripts and images

Google Search Console is the fastest way to see indexing problems. Fix those before writing another blog post.

5. Create high-quality content

Effective SEO content answers the main question, shows experience, and stays updated. Prefer one strong page per intent over many thin posts that compete with each other.

Connect related guides with internal links so readers and search engines see the relationship between topics.

6. Build a strong internal linking structure

Internal links help visitors and search engines find important pages. Use descriptive anchors, link to related guides and services, and remove orphan pages.

7. Earn quality backlinks

Relevant editorial links help discovery and authority. Publish useful resources, share expertise, and avoid buying manipulative links or joining link schemes.

8. Improve user experience and website performance

Slow pages, confusing navigation, and hard-to-read layouts hurt both users and SEO. Use responsive design, compress images, keep navigation simple, and watch Core Web Vitals as a UX signal, not a magic ranking switch.

9. Measure SEO performance with Google Search Console

To improve your SEO performance, measure it. Search Console shows clicks, impressions, CTR, average position, indexing issues, and queries.

Prioritize:

  • Pages with many impressions and low CTR (title/meta opportunity)
  • Queries around positions 11-20 (content and internal-link opportunity)
  • Indexing errors on important URLs

Evaluate weeks and months, not single-day swings. Combine Search Console with Analytics to see what visitors do after they arrive.

10. Avoid common SEO mistakes

  • Keyword stuffing
  • Near-duplicate pages without a purpose
  • Broken internal links
  • Ignoring mobile users
  • Never checking indexing
  • Spammy backlinks
  • Publishing for bots instead of people
  • Changing strategy without data

FAQ: how to improve SEO

How to improve SEO on a website?

Match intent, fix on-page clarity, clear technical blockers, strengthen internal links, and measure in Search Console. Start with pages that already appear in search.

How to improve website SEO?

Follow the checklist above. Depth on a few important URLs beats dozens of thin pages.

How to improve on-page SEO?

Unique titles and metas, one clear H1, useful headings, and content that answers the query early without stuffing keywords.

How to improve your SEO performance?

Use Search Console to find low-CTR or position 11-20 queries, improve those pages first, and track progress over weeks.

When should you hire someone to improve SEO?
