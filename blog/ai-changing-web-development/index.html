AI & Web

How AI Is Changing Web Development

By Agon Mustafaj · April 22, 2026

Web development has always evolved quickly. AI made that speed feel different. You can draft HTML structures, explore CSS patterns, generate JavaScript helpers, and review accessibility ideas in minutes. That changes the rhythm of building websites.

But AI does not remove the need for solid fundamentals. Semantic HTML, clean CSS architecture, performance, and SEO still decide whether a website feels professional.

Where AI helps most

AI is useful for scaffolding pages, suggesting interaction patterns, explaining bugs, and comparing design options. It can also support content structure and metadata planning in areas that connect web development with SEO.

Where humans still lead

Users do not experience “generated code.” They experience clarity, speed, trust, and usefulness. Choosing the right information architecture, designing meaningful interactions, and refining details still require human judgment.

The best modern workflow combines both: AI for acceleration, developers for direction. That is how I approach projects: build thoughtfully, use tools intelligently, and keep the final experience intentional.
