BANESË PËR TY

A rental platform for property discovery, search, and tenant-landlord workflows.

01. The Idea

The Idea

Banesë për Ty was created to address the rental property discovery experience, connecting tenants and landlords through a digital platform that simplifies finding, renting, and managing properties.

02. What I Built

What I Built

A full rental platform with property listings, search and filtering, rental workflows, and digital processes for contracts, signatures, and payment verification.

  • Property discovery
  • Search and filter experience
  • Rental workflows
  • Digital contracts
  • Digital signatures
  • Payment verification
  • Notifications
  • Tenant and landlord workflows
03. User Experience

User Experience

The platform is designed around two primary user types, tenants looking for properties and landlords managing listings. The experience focuses on making property discovery intuitive and rental processes streamlined through clear navigation and workflow-driven interactions.

04. Design Decisions

Design Decisions

The interface prioritizes clarity in property presentation, accessible search and filtering, and a clean layout that guides users through the rental journey, from browsing listings to completing digital agreements.

05. Technical Approach

Technical Approach

Built as a web application deployed on Netlify, focusing on responsive design and interactive client-side functionality to deliver a smooth user experience across devices.

06. SEO Perspective

SEO Perspective

The platform is structured with semantic HTML, clear page hierarchy, and descriptive content to support discoverability. Property listings and category pages are organized to provide clear signals for search engines and users alike.

07. What I Learned

What I Learned

Building Banesë për Ty reinforced the importance of designing for real user workflows, balancing feature complexity with usability, and creating digital experiences that serve both sides of a marketplace.
