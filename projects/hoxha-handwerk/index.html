01. The Idea

The Idea

Hoxha Handwerk needed a professional online presence that showcases their craftsmanship, builds trust with potential clients, and drives inquiries through a conversion-focused website experience.

02. What I Built

What I Built

A complete business website featuring a conversion-focused hero, before/after project proof, process explanations, multilingual access, and contact paths including WhatsApp, all designed to present the business professionally and convert visitors into clients.

  • Hero section
  • Multilingual interface
  • Trust and values section
  • Before/after project gallery
  • Process overview
  • Testimonials
  • FAQ section
  • Contact form
  • WhatsApp contact
  • Responsive UX
  • Conversion-oriented layout
03. User Experience

User Experience

The site guides visitors from first impression through project proof to contact. Clear visual hierarchy, before/after presentations, and social proof through testimonials create a trustworthy experience that encourages engagement.

04. Design Decisions

Design Decisions

The design emphasizes professionalism and craftsmanship. Strong hero imagery, a trust-focused layout, and a clean visual system ensure the business is presented at its best while maintaining fast load times and mobile responsiveness.

05. Technical Approach

Technical Approach

Built as a static website deployed on Netlify, optimized for performance and accessibility. The site uses semantic HTML structure and responsive CSS to deliver a consistent experience across all devices.

06. SEO Perspective

SEO Perspective

The website is structured with proper heading hierarchy, descriptive metadata, and clear content organization. Project and contact sections are designed to be crawlable and provide meaningful information for both search engines and users.

07. What I Learned

What I Learned

This project highlighted the importance of understanding a client's business goals, designing for conversion, and creating websites that serve as effective digital business cards, not just visually appealing pages.
