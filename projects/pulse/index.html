PULSE

01. The Idea

The Idea

Pulse opens as a cinematic window into deep space: NASA skies, planets in orbit, and the ISS. The goal is to make real astronomy imagery feel immediate rather than buried in a catalog.

02. What I Built

What I Built

I built a live experience around Astronomy Picture of the Day browsing (any day since June 16, 1995) with real NASA imagery, title, and explanation, plus a planets-in-orbit view using heliocentric positions from JPL SSD mean elements.

  • Astronomy Picture of the Day browser
  • Browse any day since June 16, 1995
  • NASA imagery with title and explanation
  • Planets in orbit view
  • Heliocentric positions from JPL SSD mean elements
  • Cinematic cosmos presentation
  • Cloudflare Pages deployment
03. User Experience

User Experience

The first screen sells the cosmos in motion. From there, visitors can move into APOD history or orbital views without losing the cinematic tone of the landing story.
