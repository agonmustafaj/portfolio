About Agon Mustafaj

The primary biography of a Computer Science student, Junior SEO professional, and technology builder.

Agon Mustafaj is a Computer Science student at the University of Prishtina and currently works as a Junior SEO professional at thenovalab in Pristina, Kosovo.

His professional identity spans computer science, web development, technical SEO, data analytics, and emerging technology. He is not defined by a single discipline. Rather, he brings together skills from multiple areas to build digital experiences that are well-designed, technically sound, and discoverable.

Agon is interested in web development, UI/UX design, technical SEO, data analytics, Python, Power BI, Excel, DAX, artificial intelligence, programming, and modern web technologies. He learns by building, turning ideas into real projects that demonstrate his capabilities.

Through this portfolio, Agon shares his work, skills, and ongoing learning journey. Whether you're a recruiter, developer, agency, or potential collaborator, this site is designed to show not just what he knows, but how he thinks and builds.
